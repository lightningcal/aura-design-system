AURA

The Arcade Terminal. A 1982 mainframe interface built for 2026: cyan glow on a near-black void, square corners, upper-case readouts. One set of tokens for every screen, from a phone to a television.

Principles

01

Cyan means you can act

Focus, selection and pressable things only. Never decoration, never plain text.

02

Glow, not shadow

Depth is a glow on the one focused thing and panels a shade above the void.

03

Square and flat

Every corner is zero. No gradients on controls, no blur, no glass.

04

The void is quiet

Panels separate without stacking. Hierarchy comes from type and light.

05

One canvas for TVs

1920 × 1080, scaled to the screen. One design reads the same on every TV.

06

Focus is the main state

The focused item fills cyan, glows and grows a little. Everything else waits.

07

Functional retro

Grids, notches, readouts and the now line are working parts, never costume.

Color

OKLCH, so lightness steps are even. Every role exists in both themes; switch the theme above and every swatch on this page follows. Click a swatch to copy its CSS variable.

Contrast

Every text role passes 4.5:1 on its surface. The build fails if one doesn't.

Space

Sixteen steps in TV-canvas pixels; AURA.p's xs / sm / md / lg are s2 / s4 / s8 / s10. Five named gaps set the rhythm between things.

Shape & glow

glow-dimmenus, dialogs, tooltips
glowthe focused item
glow-hoverpointer hover on a primary control
glow-dangererror banner

radius 0 Every corner. The only exception is third-party logo artwork, which keeps its own rounding.

no shadows Elevation is never a drop shadow. Text over pictures may take a soft dark text-shadow.

notch 10 × 2 L-shaped corner marks in focus cyan on framed hero art.

Motion

Short and plain. Hover or focus a tile to replay it. With reduced motion on, nothing grows, slides or pulses.

Components

Built only from tokens. Hover, tab or use the arrow keys: focus moves the way it does on a TV remote.
